# How to cancel

Cancel anytime from Settings > Billing > Plan > Cancel Subscription. You
keep access until the end of the period you already paid for; there is no
early-termination fee.

# Refund eligibility

Nimbus Cloud offers refunds within 14 days of a charge for customers who
have not exceeded 10% of their plan's usage limit in that period. Refund
requests outside this window are evaluated case by case. Contact support
with your invoice id.

# Trial cancellations

Free trials can be cancelled at any time before they convert to a paid plan
with no charge at all. If a trial converts and you cancel within 48 hours of
conversion, that first charge is refunded automatically, no request needed.

# Downgrades and proration

Downgrading from Pro to Starter takes effect at the end of the current
billing period; we do not prorate mid-cycle downgrades. If you downgrade
immediately after an annual renewal, contact support. Annual renewal
refunds for downgrades within 7 days are handled manually.

# How long refunds take

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically
appear within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
