# Plans and pricing

Nimbus Cloud offers three plans: Starter ($0/mo, up to 1,000 API calls/day),
Pro ($49/mo per seat, up to 50,000 API calls/day), and Enterprise (custom
pricing, unlimited calls, dedicated support). Annual billing gets a 15%
discount on Pro and Enterprise plans.

# Updating your payment method

Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods and click "Add card". Your new
card becomes the default for the next billing cycle immediately; you can
also set it as default right away if you want it charged on your next
invoice. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Amex.

# Understanding your invoice

Each invoice line item maps to a seat or a usage-based add-on (e.g. extra
API call bundles, extra storage). "Seat charge" means a paid team member was
active at any point during the billing period, even if only for one day. You
can download itemized PDF invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices.

# Why was I charged after cancelling?

If you cancel mid-cycle, Nimbus Cloud does not prorate the current period --
you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and the
final charge you saw was for that period, not a new one. Cancelling stops
all future renewals.

# Switching between monthly and annual billing

You can switch anytime from Settings > Billing > Plan. Switching to annual
applies the 15% discount starting the next cycle. Switching from annual to
monthly takes effect at the end of your current annual term.
